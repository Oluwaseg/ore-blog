# Beyond the Statute

An independent legal research, education, and advocacy platform advancing understanding and meaningful conversations around sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV).

Beyond the Statute explores what the law promises, what happens in practice, whose realities are overlooked, and what can make justice more accessible and meaningful. Its work includes legal research, case-law analysis, comparative studies, evidence-based commentary, public education, and survivor-centred discussion.

## What the Platform Offers

- A public-facing website for reading articles.
- Article categorization and search.
- Individual article pages.
- About, Contact, and Privacy pages.
- Newsletter subscriptions.
- Reader comments, reactions, and private story submissions for editorial review.
- An administrative dashboard for managing publications.

A custom-built, full-stack publishing platform designed to provide a simple and accessible reading and content management experience.
