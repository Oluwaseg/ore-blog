How I'd use the colours
Don't use every colour everywhere. The hierarchy matters.
Element	Colour
Main page background	Warm paper
Cards	Slightly brighter ivory
Primary buttons	Aubergine
Active navigation	Dusty rose
Headings	Deep ink
Secondary text	Warm muted grey
Dividers	Soft warm grey
Success indicators	Muted sage
Warning indicators	Muted brass
Destructive actions	Restrained red

3. A few design decisions
Typography: Keep Geist Sans for the dashboard and general UI. Use the editorial serif font for prominent public-facing headings and article titles.

For example:
<h1 className="font-heading text-5xl tracking-tight">
  Stories that deserve to be heard.
</h1>


Dashboard: Keep it functional and restrained. No unnecessary coloured cards, gradients or excessive shadows.

Public website: Give it a more editorial personality. Larger typography, generous whitespace, carefully structured article layouts and comfortable reading widths.

Dark mode: Don't just invert the light theme. The sidebar should be slightly darker than the main content, while cards should be slightly lighter.
